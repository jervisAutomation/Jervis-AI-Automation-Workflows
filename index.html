About me

Practical automation, not hype

I'm Jervis Tangoan, an AI Automation Specialist who designs and builds workflows with n8n, large language models, APIs and the business tools you already use.

My goal is simple: save people time and keep them in control. That's why my workflows check their work, avoid guessing, and ask for a human decision before anything important happens.

The projects below are working demos built to show my approach and workflow design. They are not client case studies.

How I approach every build

  • Start with the problem, not the tool
  • Keep a human approving high-stakes steps
  • Make the AI say "Unknown" instead of guessing
  • Use structured outputs so downstream steps stay reliable
  • Record a walkthrough so you can see how it works
